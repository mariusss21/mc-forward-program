Use APR to shift a mindset

Awareness, pause, and reframe. Use it in the moment, not only afterwards.

  • Awareness: notice the default reaction.
  • Pause: do not answer from that reaction.
  • Reframe: choose a more useful way to see the moment.

Problem solving

Define before you solve. Draft the Hustleton case here, then submit it on the learning platform.

Exercise 6

Your approach to problem solving

Think of a live problem at work or at home.

Seven steps, led by a hypothesis

Definition, structuring, analysis, then synthesis. The loop is the work.

  • Define the problem in one question.
  • Break it into issues without gaps or overlaps.
  • Prioritize what would change the answer.
  • Plan the analysis, the source, and the end product.
  • Do the work you planned, then stop.
  • Synthesize the so-what.
  • Recommend what should be done.

Relationships and well-being

Energy has four sources. Teams do better work when it is safe to speak and to be wrong.

Four ingredients of well-being

  • Physical: sleep, exercise, nutrition.
  • Mental: focus, hobbies, introspection.
  • Social: relationships and the role you play.
  • Spiritual: a reason that is larger than the task list.
